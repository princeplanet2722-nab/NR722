## 📌 PRODUCTION NOTES — RETENTION ENGINEERING

| Timestamp | Retention device | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Cold open on ugly manual work | Pattern interrupt — no logo, no greeting |
| 0:05 | `4 HOURS → 12 SECONDS` | End transformation shown before any explanation |
| 0:22 | Blurred #1 tool | Open curiosity loop — carries viewers to 6:30 |
| 0:30–8:00 | Hard cut every 2.5–4 seconds | Kills the "I'll come back later" impulse |
| 1:10 | 0.5s of total silence before the reveal | Silence spikes attention harder than sound |
| 2:00, 3:30, 5:00, 6:30 | 0.6s title cards | Micro-chapter reset — restarts attention span |
| 6:30 | Un-blur reveal + music drop-out | Closes the loop opened at 0:22 |
| 8:25 | Music never fades | Prevents the psychological "session over" cue |

**Edit rules:**
- No shot longer than 4 seconds without a zoom, cut, or on-screen text change.
- Every number spoken must also appear as on-screen text within 0.3 seconds.
- Zoom punch-ins on every single claim about time or money saved.
- Music: one continuous energetic bed at −22 LUFS under VO, ducking to −28 LUFS on speech.
- Captions burned in for the whole video (85% of Facebook Reels views are sound-off).

**Legal / trust:**
- Add `#ad` or "Affiliate links — costs you nothing extra, supports the channel" as the first line under the fold in the description.
- Every time-saving claim in this script should reflect your real measured result. Re-time your own runs before publishing.
